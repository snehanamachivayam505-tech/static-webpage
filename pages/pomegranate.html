Amazing Health Benefits of Pomegranate

1. Loaded with Nutrients Pomegranates are packed with fiber, vitamins, and minerals. They are especially rich in Vitamin C, Vitamin K, and potassium.
2. Powerful Antioxidants They contain punicalagins, extremely potent antioxidants found in the juice and peel that have three times the antioxidant activity of green tea.
3. Anti-Inflammatory Properties Pomegranates have potent anti-inflammatory properties, which can help reduce inflammation in the gut and throughout the body.
4. Heart Health Punicic acid, the main fatty acid in pomegranate, may help protect against several steps in the heart disease process by improving blood flow.
5. Better Memory Some evidence suggests that pomegranates may improve memory and fight against Alzheimer's disease by protecting brain cells.
