Amazing Health Benefits of Orange

1. High in Vitamin C Oranges are an excellent source of Vitamin C, which protects your cells from damage and helps your body make collagen for smoother skin.
2. Immune System Support Vitamin C is vital for the function of a healthy immune system, helping your body fight off viruses and everyday infections.
3. Heart Health The fiber, potassium, and choline in oranges all support heart health. Potassium helps regulate blood pressure and reduces the risk of stroke.
4. Lowers Cholesterol Oranges contain pectin, a soluble fiber that traps cholesterol and carries it out of the body before it's absorbed into the bloodstream.
5. Skin Protection Antioxidants in oranges help protect skin from free radical damage known to cause signs of aging, keeping your skin glowing.
