Amazing Health Benefits of Papaya

1. Aids Digestion Papaya contains an enzyme called papain that aids digestion; in fact, it can be used as a meat tenderizer.
2. High in Vitamin C Papayas are incredibly healthy fruits that are loaded with Vitamin C, which helps boost your immune system.
3. Heart Health The high fiber, potassium, and vitamin content in papaya all help to ward off heart disease.
4. Skin and Healing When used topically, crushed papaya appears to be beneficial for promoting wound healing and preventing infection of burned areas.
5. Eye Health Papaya is rich in Vitamin A, which helps protect your vision and prevents eye degeneration.
