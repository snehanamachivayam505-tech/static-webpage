Amazing Health Benefits of Guava

1. Immunity Booster Guavas are one of the richest sources of Vitamin C, containing four times the Vitamin C content found in oranges, which helps strengthen immunity.
2. Digestive Health It is an excellent source of dietary fiber. Guava seeds also serve as excellent laxatives, helping in the formation of healthy bowel movements.
3. Heart Healthy Guava helps improve the sodium and potassium balance of the body, thereby regulating blood pressure in patients with hypertension.
4. Good for Diabetes Due to its high fiber content and low glycemic index, guavas prevent the development of diabetes and help regulate blood sugar levels.
5. Skin Care Guavas are rich in Vitamin A, Vitamin C, and antioxidants like carotene and lycopene which help protect the skin from wrinkles and impurities.
